# Anatomical Measurement Technical Reference

## Overview
The anatomical measurement module (`src.measurements.measurement_engine`) calculates physical properties of segmented medical imaging structures. This document serves as a technical reference for the implemented methodologies.

## Core Concepts

### Voxel Spacing
In medical imaging, voxels are rarely isotropic (1mm x 1mm x 1mm). Real-world data often has variable spacing dependent on the scan resolution (e.g., 1.5mm x 1.5mm x 1.5mm).
Failure to account for this spacing results in inaccurate volume calculation.
The `measurement_engine` queries the NIfTI header (`img.header.get_zooms()`) to extract physical dimensions.

### Volume Measurement Methods
We calculate volume using two distinct paradigms:

#### 1. Mask-Based Volume (Highly Reliable)
Directly utilizes the 3D voxel array from the NIfTI segmentation label map.
- **Formula**: $Foreground\_Voxels \times Voxel\_Spacing\_X \times Voxel\_Spacing\_Y \times Voxel\_Spacing\_Z$
- **Pros**: Perfectly matches the output of the AI segmenter. Not dependent on surface generation algorithms. Always produces a valid result.
- **Cons**: Less granular than surface meshes.

#### 2. Mesh-Based Volume (Topology Dependent)
Calculates the volume enclosed by a 3D polygonal surface mesh generated via Marching Cubes.
- **Formula**: Integral over the closed 3D triangulated surface (`pyvista.PolyData.volume`).
- **Pros**: Incorporates sub-voxel smoothing generated by the surface extraction process.
- **Cons**: **Fails critically if the mesh is non-manifold** (contains holes, non-manifold edges, or open boundaries). For structures like the heart or aorta, Marching Cubes may create open tubular structures where PyVista's volume algorithm cannot calculate an enclosed space.

### Converting Units
The engine adheres to standard physical units for medical planning:
- 1 cubic centimeter (cm³) = 1000 cubic millimeters (mm³)
- 1 milliliter (mL) = 1 cubic centimeter (cm³)

## Structural Measurements

### Bounding Box
Calculated using the physical coordinate bounds of the 3D mesh representation.
It returns dimensions along the X, Y, and Z Cartesian axes.
- $X\_Dim = X\_max - X\_min$
- $Y\_Dim = Y\_max - Y\_min$
- $Z\_Dim = Z\_max - Z\_min$

### Euclidean Distance
A mathematical utility used to compute straight-line distance between two 3D spatial points in millimeter space. Useful for simple annotations, but does not follow anatomical curvature or geodesics.

## Safety & Limitations
Measurements are derived from predictive AI output and mathematical estimations. They are designed for educational reference and surgical planning mockups.
**They must never be used as the sole determinant for clinical or diagnostic decisions.**
